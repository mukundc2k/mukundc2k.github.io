  translation feels solved until you ask what's still hard about it. LTB is a live benchmark of 3,456 multimodal examples across 109 languages, each with its own verification rules, plus a source-side taxonomy of what makes an example difficult, both linguistic and extra-linguistic. i proposed and built the taxonomy during my summer at ETH Zürich. the twist: hand models the rules and they pass, so they can follow constraints, they just can't spot them on their own.
