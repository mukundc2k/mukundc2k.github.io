abstract: >-
  can LLMs pick up an abstract hierarchical pattern and carry it forward, and how do they compare to everyone else who can? we use an artificial grammar learning task (hierarchical vs. linear stimuli, from abstract to semantic) and bayesian mixture modeling over what gets generated next, to infer whether each completion is best explained as hierarchical, linear, random, or unparsable. we run this across four LLM generations and several families, and put them on the same plot as crows, monkeys, children, and adults. early results place LLMs on a continuum alongside biological intelligences rather than off to one side. this is the accepted pilot, kept as an abstract; the full version is being expanded for a journal.
